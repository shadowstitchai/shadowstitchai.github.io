Coutto Pattern supports projector cutting, stitching, printing, managing, and storing patterns—all in one app.

Built for sewists, makers, and embroidery enthusiasts: import, organize, view, calibrate, project, nest, and print in one workspace. Import PDF, SVG, DXF, PLT, PNG, JPEG, and WebP, plus embroidery formats such as DST, PES, JEF, and EXP.

- **Projector cutting**: Calibrate quickly, then cut at true size on fabric. Recolor, move, and rotate patterns — light or dark fabric.
- **Tiled printing**: Print large PLT, DXF, and PDF patterns as A4/A3 tiles straight from your phone — no conversion needed.
- **Pattern stitching**: Join A4 pages with matched edges, ready to print or project and cut.
- **Pattern library**: View patterns directly, sort by style or format, and back up to the cloud so you can restore on another device.
- **Image calibration**: Calibrate PNG and JPG pattern sizes so image patterns project at true scale.
- **Embroidery viewing**: Open embroidery files and preview stitch playback.
- **Sewing community**: Find designers you love, and discover patterns, tutorials, and fabrics.
