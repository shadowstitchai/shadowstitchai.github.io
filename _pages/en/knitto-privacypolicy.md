**Effective date: August 13, 2026**

Beijing Shadow Stitch Technology Co., Ltd. ("**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use **Knitto Stitch** (the "**App**").

Knitto Stitch is a pocket stitch cookbook with photo-guided lessons for 27 classic knitting stitches, plus search, favorites, and a row counter. We designed Knitto around a "local-first, collect-nothing by default" principle: your data stays on your device by default.

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect your personal information, and we do not track your usage.**

- The App does not require an account or sign-in
- The App does not integrate advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs
- The App does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information

## 2. How We Handle Your Data

All data you create while using the App is stored locally on your device:

| Data type | Description | Storage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knitting projects and row counters | Counters and progress records you create | On-device (app support directory) |
| Stitch favorites | Stitches you favorite | On-device |
| App settings | Preferences such as language and appearance | On-device (system settings storage) |

We do not upload this data to servers, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any form of tracking.

## 3. Permissions

Knitto does not require sensitive permissions such as photos, camera, contacts, or location to function, and it does not request them.

## 4. Network and Third-Party Services

Knitto does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. The stitch content in the App is bundled with the App and does not require an internet connection.

## 5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal information. Because we do not collect personal information, there are no scenarios in which we share personal information with third parties.

## 6. Security

Your data is stored on your device and protected by the mechanisms provided by your device's operating system. Please keep your device secure and up to date.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general users. If you are under 18, please use the App with guardian guidance.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through in-app notices, website announcements, or other reasonable means. Continued use of Knitto after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
